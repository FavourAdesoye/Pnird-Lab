PNIRD Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026 · Contact: www.pnird.com

PNIRD Lab (“we,” “our,” or “the Lab”) provides a mobile application for Virginia State University’s psychology research community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who this app is for

The app is used by lab staff and community members (such as students and participants) to share lab updates, browse studies and events, message staff, and use optional lab tools (including a lab information chatbot and cognitive games). It is not an electronic health record or clinical care system.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, role (staff or community), optional bio and profile photo.
  • Authentication: sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (we do not store your password in our app database).
  • User content: posts, comments, likes, direct messages, and study/event content you create or interact with.
  • Chatbot data: questions and replies if you use the lab chatbot (may be retained for a limited period for history).
  • Technical data: basic device and diagnostic information needed to keep the app reliable.

3. How we use information

  • Provide accounts, profiles, posts, studies, events, messaging, and notifications.
  • Authenticate users and enforce staff vs community permissions.
  • Answer lab-related questions via the chatbot.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication
  • MongoDB — application database
  • Cloudinary — image hosting
  • OpenAI — lab chatbot responses (your chatbot messages may be sent to OpenAI to generate answers)

Please avoid sharing sensitive health, research, or personal data in chats or the chatbot.

5. Retention

Account and community content are kept while your account is active. Chatbot conversation history, when saved, is retained for a limited period (typically about 30 days) and then deleted automatically. You can also delete chatbot history and messages from the app’s Privacy settings.

6. Your choices

  • Update your profile information in the app.
  • Delete messages, notifications, and chatbot history from Privacy settings.
  • Delete your entire account from Settings → Delete Account (removes your login and personal app data we control).
  • Contact us via www.pnird.com for privacy questions.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for university community use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
